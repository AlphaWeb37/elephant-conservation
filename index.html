Why Elephants Matter

Elephants are more than the world's largest land mammals. They are nature's engineers, shaping ecosystems and supporting countless forms of life.

🌳 Forest Gardeners

Elephants spread seeds across forests, helping plants grow and maintaining biodiversity.

💧 Water Engineers

During droughts they dig for underground water, creating water sources for many other animals.

🌍 Ecosystem Protectors

Their movement shapes landscapes and creates natural pathways used by wildlife.

❤️ Gentle Giants

Elephants form strong family bonds, protect their young and display remarkable intelligence.

Did You Know?

🧠 Amazing Memory

Elephants can remember family members and important migration routes for many years.

👂 Excellent Hearing

Their large ears help them detect distant sounds and regulate body temperature.

❤️ Strong Families

Elephants live in close family groups and protect their calves throughout their lives.

🌍 Three Species

There are three living species: African Savanna, African Forest and Asian elephants.

🌿 Huge Appetite

An adult elephant can eat over 150 kilograms of vegetation every single day.

🗣️ Long-Distance Communication

Low-frequency rumbles allow elephants to communicate across several kilometres.

🏊 Excellent Swimmers

Elephants enjoy swimming and use their trunks as natural snorkels.

💤 Surprisingly Little Sleep

Wild elephants often sleep only two to four hours a day.
